Revision should produce answers, not highlighted notes.

Someone who already knows ML does not need to relearn every course. The goal is to retrieve ideas without support, connect them to scenarios, and explain trade-offs under questioning.

Retrieval check

Without notes, explain why accuracy can be misleading on imbalanced data. Give one business example and one better evaluation approach.

The four-step revision cycle

1

Recall

Explain the topic from memory before opening a resource.

2

Repair

Study only the missing or incorrect part.

3

Apply

Use it on an example, comparison or small calculation.

4

Speak

Give a concise answer aloud and handle one follow-up.

Why it works: interviews require retrieval and explanation. Re-reading creates familiarity, but familiarity can feel like knowledge even when the idea cannot be produced independently.
