A three-hour career workshop

Prepare for the interview, not just the syllabus.

ML interviews test more than algorithm definitions. A candidate must identify the right roles, explain concepts clearly, defend project decisions, design complete systems, solve coding problems, and communicate credible evidence.

Why this session is personal

I used versions of this preparation system while preparing for and clearing interview processes at Qualcomm, HARMAN, Amazon and Walmart. The process changed between roles, but the demanding parts stayed remarkably similar.

What actually made the difference: not one clever answer or a secret question list, but repeated revision, explaining concepts aloud, defending project decisions, practising coding, sitting through uncomfortable mocks and correcting the weaknesses they exposed.

This experience does not guarantee another person the same outcome. It does provide a tested preparation process that can be adapted, measured and improved.

Opening question

If an interview were scheduled for tomorrow, which stage would expose your biggest gap: resume screening, ML concepts, project discussion, coding, system design, or behaviour?
