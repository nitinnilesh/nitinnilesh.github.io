Act 2 · Foundation · 9 minutes

Every prediction has two stories.

What the model predicted—and what actually happened. Cross those two questions and four outcomes appear.

Build it, do not memorize it

  1. Choose the class we care about: fraud = positive.
  2. Ask: was the prediction positive or negative?
  3. Ask: was it correct (true) or wrong (false)?
True / false means correct / incorrect. Positive / negative means the model’s predicted class.

Audience check

The transaction was fraud, but the model said legitimate. What is it?

False Negative. The model predicted negative, and that prediction was false.
