This course is organized as a sequence of visual teaching packs. The explanations emphasize intuition first, then connect that intuition to equations, algorithms, evaluation choices, and practical modeling decisions.

The current release contains browser-based course pages only. Notebooks, datasets, assignments, recordings, and downloadable course archives are intentionally excluded and may be added separately later.
